### Methodology

The optimization framework consists of four coupled stages:

1. Base Card Allocation via Order Statistics: The first 50 cards are assigned values matching the extreme lower quantile (0.0001 probability level) of the k-th order statistic of 50 uniform random variables on [L, U]. This ensures that each pile receives a massive base value close to its target without exceeding B_k.

2. Redundant Geometric Denominations: The remaining 450 delta cards are partitioned into 40 geometric denominations with ratio r = 1.33 starting at 2.0 * 10^12 down to ~2.95 * 10^7. The redundancy ratio r = 1.33 allows pairs of smaller cards to substitute for single larger cards, eliminating bit-level bottlenecks across all 50 piles simultaneously.

3. Best-Fit Decreasing Initialization: Base cards are matched against target values with a greedy fallback verification. Delta cards are subsequently assigned in descending order of value using best-fit greedy insertion.

4. Time-Adaptive Simulated Annealing: The search engine executes simulated annealing across four move types: single-card transfers, 1-for-1 card swaps, 2-for-1 swaps, and 2-for-2 swaps. The execution loop dynamically tracks high-resolution clock time, executing restarts until 1.70 seconds elapse, retaining the best assignment encountered.
