### Methodology

The optimization framework comprises four integrated stages:

1. High-Safety Order-Statistic Base Allocation: The first 50 cards are computed using the 0.00001 probability quantile of the k-th order statistic of 50 uniform random variables on [L, U]. This prevents base cards from exceeding designated pile targets B_k, preserving an identity match on every instance.

2. Redundant Geometric Delta Progression: The remaining 450 delta cards follow a geometric progression with ratio r = 1.33 across 40 denominations from 2.0 * 10^12 down to ~2.95 * 10^7. The ratio r = 1.33 ensures overlapping coverage so that no single pile experiences a shortage of representational capacity.

3. Best-Fit Decreasing Initialization: Base cards are placed with an identity mapping, and delta cards are assigned from largest to smallest value using best-fit greedy insertion into piles with remaining headroom.

4. Calibrated Temperature Simulated Annealing: The annealing engine operates with an initial temperature of t_start = 2.0 * 10^7 cooling geometrically to t_end = 0.1 over 6,000,000 iterations per restart. This initial temperature allows fluid restructuring of delta cards up to the 5.0 * 10^7 scale while preventing disruption of high-order card assignments. An outer timer loop runs restarts continuously until 1.70 seconds elapse per testcase, returning the best configuration found.
