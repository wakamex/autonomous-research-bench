### Methodology

The optimization framework comprises four integrated techniques:

1. High-Safety Order-Statistic Base Allocation: The first 50 cards are assigned values matching the 0.00001 probability quantile of the k-th order statistic of 50 uniform random variables on [L, U]. This stricter safety margin lowers the probability of a base card exceeding its designated pile target B_k to virtually zero across all public test instances, avoiding expensive fallback reallocations.

2. Redundant Geometric Delta Progression: The remaining 450 delta cards are distributed across 40 geometric denominations with ratio r = 1.33 starting at 2.0 * 10^12 down to ~2.95 * 10^7. The ratio r = 1.33 ensures that any single denomination shortage in a pile can be compensated by pairs of cards from lower denominations without causing error cascades.

3. Best-Fit Decreasing Initialization: Base cards are placed with an identity mapping and verified against target values. Delta cards are subsequently assigned in descending order of value using greedy best-fit insertion to minimize initial residuals.

4. Slower-Cooling Time-Adaptive Simulated Annealing: The search engine executes 6,000,000 iterations per restart across four neighborhood moves: single-card transfers, 1-for-1 card swaps, 2-for-1 swaps, and 2-for-2 swaps. The slower cooling rate per restart allows deeper exploration of the discrete combinatorial landscape, while the outer timer loop runs restarts continuously until 1.70 seconds elapse per testcase, keeping the best assignment found.
