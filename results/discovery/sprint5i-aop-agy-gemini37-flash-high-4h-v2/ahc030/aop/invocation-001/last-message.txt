### Motivation and Question

Can we minimize total query cost in the AHC030 polyomino oil mining challenge through exact constraint propagation, high-capacity branch-and-bound backtracking, and targeted Markov Chain Monte Carlo posterior estimation?

### Core Algorithmic Architecture

The final solver in [solution.cpp](file:///code/ARS/benchmarks/ahc030/solution.cpp) integrates four synchronized algorithmic layers:

1. Bidirectional Arc Consistency Engine
After every drill, constraint propagation evaluates placement feasibility:
- Upper bound core pruning: if the number of polyominoes guaranteed to cover a cell matches the drilled oil quantity, any other placement covering that cell is pruned.
- Lower bound capacity pruning: if the maximum possible overlapping polyominoes equals the drilled oil quantity, candidate shapes are required to cover that cell.
- Bitset zero-drilled filtering: any placement overlapping a cell with zero oil is immediately eliminated.

2. Polyomino Symmetry Breaking and Exact DFS
- Identical polyomino shapes are grouped into equivalence classes, enforcing non-decreasing placement index ordering to prune factorial symmetric permutations.
- When the state space is under 2,000,000 configurations, branch-and-bound DFS evaluates upper bound feasibility and lower bound remaining capacity.
- If all valid configurations share identical oil presence, the solution is submitted immediately with zero further probes.
- When multiple configurations remain, the probe selects the cell that maximizes the information-theoretic entropy split `p * (1 - p)`.

3. Multi-Particle Targeted MCMC Posterior Estimation
- For large state spaces, an ensemble of 70 state particles evolves via Metropolis-Hastings transitions.
- Targeted proposal moves steer particles toward unsatisfied cells, adding shapes to deficit cells and moving shapes away from excess cells.
- If particle agreement on oil presence reaches consensus, the solver submits the answer immediately.

4. Strategic Active Unexplained Oil Tracking and Staggered Exploration
- Unexplained oil tracking: 4-neighbor expansion bonuses are restricted to positive drilled cells not yet fully accounted for by uniquely placed shapes, preventing wasted drills around finished shapes.
- Staggered spatial repulsion: during initial exploration when no positive cells are known, probe points adjacent to known zero-cells are penalized, enforcing an efficient spatial lattice.
- Global oil conservation: the moment the sum of drilled positive cells matches the known total polyomino area, all remaining unrevealed cells are proven to have zero oil, enabling instantaneous answer submission.
