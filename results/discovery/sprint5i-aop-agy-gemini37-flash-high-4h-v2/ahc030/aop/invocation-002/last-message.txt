### Core Algorithmic Architecture

The optimized solver in [solution.cpp](file:///code/ARS/benchmarks/ahc030/solution.cpp) integrates four interacting subsystems:

1. Full Arc Consistency Engine
After every single drill, constraint propagation updates all polyomino placement domains:
- Zero-oil filtering: all placements overlapping any zero-drilled cell are pruned.
- Upper bound core pruning: when the total core-guaranteed oil across polyominoes equals the drilled oil count at cell `u`, all non-core placements covering `u` are pruned.
- Lower bound capacity pruning: when the maximum possible overlapping candidate shapes equals the drilled oil count at cell `u`, candidate shapes are forced to cover `u`.

2. Exact Symmetry-Broken DFS
- Polyomino shapes with identical geometry are grouped into symmetry classes, enforcing monotonic placement indexing to prune factorial redundant branches.
- When the Cartesian product of valid placements is under 2,000,000 configurations, branch-and-bound DFS evaluates exact solutions.
- If all valid solutions share identical oil presence, the answer is submitted immediately.
- If ambiguity remains, the probe selects the cell maximizing the binary entropy `p * (1 - p)`.

3. Scaled 90-Particle Targeted MCMC Posterior Estimation
- When the state space exceeds 2,000,000 configurations, an ensemble of 90 state particles explores the posterior distribution.
- Metropolis-Hastings transitions use targeted proposals directed at unsatisfied cells, adding candidate shapes to deficit cells and moving shapes away from excess cells.
- When consensus among valid particles exceeds 98% certainty across all cells, the solver submits the answer immediately.

4. Active Unexplained Oil Tracking and Staggered Exploration
- Unexplained oil tracking: 4-neighbor expansion bonuses are granted only to drilled positive cells that are not yet explained by uniquely determined polyominoes. This eliminates wasted exploration around finished shapes.
- Staggered spatial repulsion: during initial exploration when no positive cells are known, probes adjacent to known zero cells are penalized, enforcing an efficient lattice over the grid.
- Global conservation termination: as soon as the total oil discovered matches the known total polyomino area, all remaining unrevealed cells are proven to be zero, enabling immediate answer submission.
